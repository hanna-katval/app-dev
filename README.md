#The Notebook (2004)
##About the movie
**The Notebook** is a romantic drama movie released in 2004, that was directed by Nick Cassavetes and based on the novel written by Nicholas Sparks. It tells the love story of *Noah Calhoun* and *Allie Hamilton* who fall in love despite their differences.
---
##Main Characters
- **Noah Calhoun** - a hardworking young man who believes in true love.
- **Allie Hamilton** - a kind and independent woman who follows her heart.
